09 · Privacy

What we collect (and what we don’t)

This site is a static brochure. We do not set cookies, and we do not use browser local storage or session storage for tracking or ads.

Analytics. We use Plausible Analytics (privacy-focused, cookieless). Plausible does not use cookies or persistent identifiers; it does not store personal data intended to identify individuals. It records aggregate measures such as pageviews, referrers, device type, and outbound link clicks (for example to Cal.com or LinkedIn). See Plausible’s data policy.

Hosting. Pages are served from AWS (S3 + CloudFront). Edge servers process standard request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) for delivery, security, and operational logs. We do not use that pipeline for advertising profiles.

When you leave this site. Links to Cal.com, LinkedIn, Instagram, and other third parties are outside our control. Those services may set cookies or collect data under their own policies once you open them.

Site UI script. Our small /js/main.js only handles navigation (mobile menu and section highlighting). It does not set cookies or call third-party trackers.

Full policy. estesadvisory.com/privacy.html — plain-language notice for sites, products, partners, and developer apps (e.g. LinkedIn). Not a substitute for formal legal advice.
