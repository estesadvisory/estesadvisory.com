Estes Advisory Launches Artifactum Hosted with Universal MCP Support and Dual-Track Onboarding

A private, cross-model context vault allowing engineering and operations teams to share durable project memory across Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Grok, and Hermes Agent.

AUSTIN, Texas & SAN FRANCISCO — Estes Advisory LLC announces the general early-access release of Artifactum Hosted (artifactum.ai), an isolated context vault engineered for teams navigating the multi-model AI era.

The Challenge: AI Context Fragmentation

Modern technical organizations rarely rely on a single AI provider. Engineers write code in Cursor, brainstorm architecture in Claude, execute research in ChatGPT, and run autonomous scripts in Hermes Agent. However, each provider operates as a walled garden: project specifications, architectural decisions, and working state remain trapped within ephemeral chat threads. When context limits are reached or developers switch tools, critical continuity is lost.

The Solution: Open Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Artifactum resolves cross-tool amnesia by providing dedicated, private storage accessible to any AI via open Model Context Protocol (MCP) over standard Server-Sent Events (SSE) and HTTP transports with OAuth 2.0 PKCE authentication. Rather than managing sprawling chat logs, AI agents and developers interact with canonical Content Objects: durable drafts, architecture decision records (ADRs), and session handovers.

Universal Compatibility Verified

Artifactum Hosted is verified and working across major daily AI surfaces:

  • Claude (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, and Claude.ai custom connectors)
  • Cursor IDE (Native MCP integration)
  • ChatGPT Plus (Developer mode on the web)
  • Grok (Grok Bot, Grok web/iOS, and Grok Build CLI)
  • Hermes Agent (CLI v0.21.1+ native MCP tool calling)
  • Any MCP-Compatible Client (Universal HTTP/SSE endpoints with Bearer authentication)

Dual-Track Availability: Self-Serve & Assisted Onboarding

To meet teams where they are, Artifactum is launching with dual conversion tracks:

  1. Instant Hosted Subscriptions: Self-serve plans starting at $10/month for Individuals, $49/month for Teams (10 seats), and $300/month for Business (100 seats) with automated provisioning and passwordless email OTP access.
  2. Estes Advisory Assisted Implementation: A fixed-scope technical onboarding package for founders and teams requiring white-glove setup. Includes workflow inventory, custom vault structuring, multi-client connection, project context migration, and a 14-day dedicated async support window.

Estes Advisory Establishes Public Announcements and Product Release Wire

A durable, first-party destination for company milestones, technical architecture updates, and product releases across Artifactum, ForgeLens, and open-source systems.

AUSTIN, Texas & SAN FRANCISCO — Estes Advisory LLC announces the establishment of its official public announcements wire at estesadvisory.com/press.html. Designed as a durable, first-party record of company progress, the wire hosts formal product releases, architectural milestones, and partnership announcements directly on company-owned infrastructure rather than relying solely on third-party social distribution.

As Estes Advisory expands its portfolio of software products alongside its enterprise advisory practice, the wire provides partners, technical leaders, and investors with stable, canonical reference links for all major releases. The wire chronicles progress across key portfolio surfaces:

  • Artifactum: The MCP-native Content Object vault for autonomous agents and engineering teams, delivering durable, hierarchical object storage with private AWS posture.
  • ForgeLens: Digital twin and reality-capture verification systems for construction and field operations, uniting BIM data, reality capture, and AI-assisted checks.
  • Open Source Tooling: Public adapters and protocols including grok-a2a, an open-source A2A wrapper for xAI Grok.
  • Advisory & Practice Milestones: Enterprise cloud architecture, data platform direction, and strategic delivery network updates.

All future releases follow a structured, verifiable format with permanent anchor links, metadata suitable for professional social sharing across LinkedIn and X, and direct access to media and executive contacts.
